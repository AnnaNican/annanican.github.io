In Crampons to the Museum

A yearlong newsletter as an attempt to examine ideas and remembrance to marvel at so many concepts, phenomena, objects, and people

For one year(only) I wrote a newsletter that I have clumsily titled “In Crampons to the Museum”. In hindsight, given the course of 2020, I should have called it “With books in athleisure”. That year there was no mountain climbing and the museums (at least in New York) were shut down for the majority of spring and summer. Yet I had no shortage of content that I wanted to share.
