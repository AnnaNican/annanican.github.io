Using linear optimization for day-to-day things

In 2015, I spent a lot of time programming linear optimizers for media spend. To demonstrate its practicality, I created a project that applied this approach to daily decisions, from choosing vacation destinations to picking out books. I showcased this work at PyData and PyDx, sharing the concept and its applications.
