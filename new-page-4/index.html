The list of beautiful things that i would take from the Metropolitan Museum of Art if I could

I was really inspired by the book "All the Beauty in the world" by Patrick Bringley. In the last chapter, as the author eventually changes his job, he contemplates “all he could carry out with him”. Intrigued, I decided to design my fanciful escapade to explore the hidden gems of the MET. So, here is my project “If I could take it with me”, where I invite you to embark with me on an imaginary heist, seeking humanity's most splendid creations.
